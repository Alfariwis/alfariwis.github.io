How to delete your Kulliñ data

Kulliñ: walk and explore Chile · developed by Alfariwis · Last updated: November 19, 2026

Your game lives on your phone and, if you use Google Play Games, a copy is saved to your Google account. If you turned on Friends, your cabin is also saved in Firebase, a Google Cloud service. You can delete everything yourself, without waiting for anyone. If you prefer, you can also ask us by email (step 4).

1. Delete your Friends account

  1. In Kulliñ, open Settings → Friends.
  2. Tap Delete my Friends account and confirm.

Deleted from the servers immediately: your cabin (with your steps for the week), your friend code, your friend list, your requests, who you blocked and your Firebase account. You're removed from your friends' lists. On your phone, the streak with your friends and its photo are also deleted; the rest of your game doesn't change.

2. Delete the data on your phone

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Kulliñ.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear data. Or simply uninstall Kulliñ.

Deleted immediately: your pet, discovered species, coins, objects, achievements saved on the phone, the game's step counts and your year of birth.

3. Delete the copy in Google Play Games

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or Google Play → your photo → Play Games).
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Under «Delete individual game data», find Kulliñ and tap Delete.

Deleted: the game saved in the cloud (progress, the game's step counts and year of birth), achievements and your scores on Kulliñ's Play Games leaderboards.

If you have Android backup turned on, you can also delete the phone's backup from Google Drive → Backups.

4. Ask us by email

Write to nicolasf600@gmail.com with the subject «Delete my Kulliñ data». We'll reply within 30 days at most. If you can't get into the game, tell us your friend code and we'll delete your Friends account; for everything else, we'll guide you to delete what's on your phone and in your Google account.

What data is kept

  • Kulliñ (Alfariwis) keeps none of your data after you delete your Friends account, except abuse reports made about you, which are kept for 6 months to review abuse and are then deleted by the reporter's game.
  • Technical data that Google AdMob collects when showing a video ad (for example, device identifiers and information about the ads) is managed by Google and kept according to its retention policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
