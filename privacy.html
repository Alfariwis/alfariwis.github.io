Kulliñ privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Kulliñ is an educational game about the flora, fauna and funga of Chile: you walk with your virtual pet and discover species. This policy explains what data the game uses, what for, and how you can control it. The Spanish version has the same content.

1. Who is responsible

Kulliñ is developed by Alfariwis, from Chile, who is responsible for the data the game processes. Contact: nicolasf600@gmail.com.

This policy applies in every country where Kulliñ is published, including the European Economic Area (General Data Protection Regulation), the United Kingdom, the United States and Chile (Law 19,628 and, once in force, Law 21,719).

2. What data the game uses and why

  • Steps. Kulliñ reads your step count from Health Connect («Read steps» permission) and from the phone's physical activity sensor. It uses them only for the game: to turn your walks into expeditions, sightings and coins. We don't read any other health data, we don't write to Health Connect, and we don't use your steps for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.
  • Year of birth. When you start, the game asks what year you were born. We use it to show ads suited to your age (section 4) and to know whether Friends is available (section 5).
  • Game progress. Your pet, discovered species, coins and achievements are stored on your phone. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Google Play Games backup. If you use Google Play Games, a copy of your game is saved to your Google account, in the Google Play Games saved games service, so you can continue on another phone. The copy includes your progress, the game's step counts and your year of birth. The game's achievements are also recorded in Google Play Games. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Android backup. If you have it turned on, Android may include the game's data in your Google account backup.
  • Google Play Games leaderboards. If you turn on Friends (section 3), the game sends two numbers to Google Play Games: the steps you took with Kulliñ that week and how many species you've seen. They appear on Kulliñ's leaderboards next to your Play Games profile, which other players can see depending on the visibility you chose in Play Games.

3. Friends

Friends is optional and stays off until you turn it on. It's only available from age 13 (section 5).

  • How it works. So your friends can see your cabin, Kulliñ saves a copy of it in Cloud Firestore, a Google Cloud (Firebase) service, on servers located in Santiago, Chile. You sign in with your Google Play Games account; the game doesn't know your name or email.
  • What is saved. Your pet's name (with a word filter, or just the species if you choose), its species, model, color, stage and scarf; your cabin's decoration and objects; the tricks it knows; the species in your guide and how many times you saw each one; your course records; how the pet of the friend you completed your last streak with looks (without its name); the steps you took with Kulliñ this week and last week, and your weekly average (only those totals, for the streak with a friend); the total steps you've walked with your pet; and the date of the last update. Your friend code, your friend list, sent and received requests, and who you blocked are also saved. An identifier provided by Google Play Games is used to identify your account.
  • Who sees it. Only the players you accepted as friends see your cabin, your steps for the week and your total steps. Someone with your friend code sees only your pet's name, species and stage, to decide whether to send you a request. If a friend completes a streak with you, their friends can also see how your pet looks, without its name, in the photo in their cabin. Nobody sees your name, email or location, and Kulliñ has no chat and no photo uploads.
  • Streak with a friend. Each week you can choose a friend to add their steps to yours. Who you chose, your rewards and the weeks in a row are stored only on your phone and in your game backup. When you complete the streak, a photo of the two pets, drawn by the game, stays in your cabin. How your friend's pet looks (species, stage, model, color and scarf, without its name) is saved with your cabin, and your friends see it when they visit you. From version 1.0.22, the photo is removed when you remove or block that friend; if they remove you or delete their Friends account, it's removed the next time your game connects. Your friend gets no notification: they only see your step totals, like any friend.
  • Play Games friends. Your Google Play Games friends who also play Kulliñ with Friends turned on may see you as a suggestion and send you a request. For that, your Play Games identifier is saved next to your friend code. To see your own suggestions, Play Games asks your permission to share your friend list with Kulliñ; that list isn't saved.
  • Invitation link. The link you share carries your friend code after the # sign, which the browser never sends to any server. If the person who receives it installs Kulliñ from that page, Google Play passes your code to the game to show them the invitation.
  • Reports. If you report someone, who reported whom and when is saved, only to review abuse. Reports are kept for 6 months: after that, the reporter's game deletes them. They're also deleted if that person deletes their Friends account.
  • Adult PIN. For players aged 13 to 17, an adult chooses a PIN when turning on Friends. The PIN is stored only on the phone, encrypted, and is never sent.

4. Ads

  • Kulliñ shows video ads only when you choose to watch them, in exchange for an in-game benefit.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, a certified provider for family apps.
  • Ads seen by players under 18, or whose age we don't know, are suitable for all audiences. Adults may be shown ads suitable for teens and older (T rating).
  • Under 18, or if we don't know your age: ads are not personalized, they're marked as directed to minors, and the phone's advertising ID isn't used.
  • 18 and over: ads may be personalized. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. In the United States, you can opt out of personalized ads ("sale" or "sharing" of data under state privacy laws) from the privacy message the game shows, or by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • AdMob may collect technical device data and information about the ads shown in order to deliver and measure them and prevent fraud, according to Google's policy.

5. Children

  • Kulliñ is suitable for all ages.
  • Friends isn't available to children under 13 or to anyone who didn't give their age: the game doesn't send any server data from a child under 13 to show to others.
  • From 13 to 17, Friends is turned on only with the PIN chosen by an adult, who can also turn it off with that PIN.
  • Where the law requires it (for example, in some US states), when you open Friends the game asks Google Play for the account's age range and whether a parent approved. That signal is used only to decide whether Friends is available: it isn't stored on servers or used for ads.
  • For children under 14 (and, as a precaution, for everyone under 18) ads aren't personalized and the advertising ID isn't used.
  • We don't ask children for any personal data other than their year of birth, which is used for the above and to know whether Friends is available.
  • Parents and guardians can write to us at nicolasf600@gmail.com with any question.

6. Who we share data with

We don't sell data. Only the Google services described above are involved: Health Connect on your phone, Google Play Games, Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication) and AdMob. With Friends turned on, your cabin and your steps for the week are seen by the friends you accepted.

7. Where data is stored

  • Your game, your steps and your year of birth are stored on your phone.
  • Friends data is stored in Cloud Firestore, on Google servers located in Santiago, Chile.
  • Google Play Games, Firebase Authentication and AdMob may process data on Google servers in other countries, such as the United States.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom, your data leaves those areas. These transfers are made under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, included in the Firebase and Google data processing terms.

8. Why we can use your data

  • Steps. With your express permission, which you give by accepting the Health Connect or sensor step permission. You can withdraw it at any time (section 11).
  • Game progress and year of birth. Because they're needed to give you the game you asked for, with ads and features suited to your age.
  • Friends and the weekly steps your friends see. With your consent, which you give by turning on Friends. From 13 to 17, an adult also gives it with their PIN. You can withdraw it by turning off Friends or deleting your Friends account.
  • Personalized ads. Only with your consent, for players 18 and over. Non-personalized ads are shown because they help keep the game free, and only when you choose to watch them.
  • Reports. To prevent abuse and protect players.

Kulliñ doesn't make automated decisions with legal effects on you or build advertising profiles from your steps.

9. How long we keep it

  • What's on your phone is kept until you uninstall Kulliñ.
  • Friends data is kept while you have your Friends account, until you delete it.
  • Reports are kept for 6 months.
  • The copy in Google Play Games is kept until you delete it from Play Games.

10. Your rights

  • You can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive it in a format you can take with you (portability), object to its use or ask for it to be restricted.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • Many of these rights you can exercise directly in the game (section 11). For anything else, write to us at nicolasf600@gmail.com. We reply within one month.
  • If you think we don't comply, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country: for example, in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es); in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in Chile, the Personal Data Protection Agency, once it starts operating.

11. How to delete your data

  • To delete everything about Friends, in the game go to Settings → Friends → «Delete my Friends account». Your cabin (with your steps for the week), your code, your list, your requests and your Firebase account are deleted from the servers immediately, and you're removed from your friends' lists. On your phone, the streak with your friends and its photo are also deleted. Your friends' phones may keep the last copy of your cabin they saw, and a friend's cabin may keep your pet in a streak photo, until their game connects again (the photo, from version 1.0.22).
  • Turning off Friends stops sending data, but doesn't delete it: that's what the previous option is for.
  • Uninstalling Kulliñ deletes the data stored on your phone.
  • To delete the cloud copy, your achievements and your leaderboard scores, go to Google Play Games → Settings → «Delete Play Games account & data» (or just Kulliñ's data).
  • You can remove the step permission at any time from Health Connect or from the app's settings in Android.
  • Step by step, and what data is deleted: How to delete your data.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit when sent to Google services. In Firebase, security rules prevent anyone from reading a cabin without being its owner's friend, or from changing another player's data.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll publish the new version on this same page with its date.
